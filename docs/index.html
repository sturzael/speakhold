Speakhold.

Two things, one small Mac app. Dictation: hold a key, talk, let go. Meeting notes: press record on a call, get the notes when it ends.

Whisper runs on the Mac and the audio never leaves it. No bot joins your calls. The notes are written by Claude Code under your own plan, your own key, or Apple Intelligence.

What it does

Dictation

Hold a chord, speak, let go. The sentence is pasted into whatever app is in front, cleaned up. Replaces Willow Voice, Wispr Flow and Superwhisper.

Meeting notes

Press record on a Zoom, Meet or Teams call. It records both sides on your Mac, stops when the call ends, and writes the notes and the transcript to one file. Replaces Fireflies and the other bots. How it works.

Why people switch from Willow, Wispr Flow and Superwhisper

Hold, talk, let go

A small tab docked to the screen edge opens into a short bar of glowing waveform while you hold the key. Let go and the sentence lands at the cursor.

100% on-device

Speech-to-text runs with WhisperKit on the Apple Neural Engine. Works offline. Audio never leaves the machine.

Any hotkey chord

Left and right modifiers are different keys. Default is left ⌃ + left ⌥, which collides with nothing. Hold to talk or press to toggle.

Pastes anywhere

Slack, Gmail, Xcode, Terminal, Notes, the browser. Wherever the cursor is. Your clipboard is restored afterwards.

Native, no Electron

One Swift binary, a 12 MB download. No Electron, no Tauri, no background helpers. Lives in the menu bar; the Dock icon is optional.

Polish on your Mac

On macOS 26, Apple Intelligence removes fillers, fixes punctuation and spells names from your dictionary, on-device, no key. Prefer Claude? Add your own key. Spoken commands and text replacements always work.

Undo, lock, retry

Undo sits on the bar for three seconds after a paste. Tap Space while holding to go hands-free. Try again re-runs a failed recording.

Takes the minutes

Record both sides of a Zoom, Meet or Teams call, no bot and no virtual driver. It stops itself when the call ends; the notes are written in the background and a notification opens the file. Audio never leaves the Mac. How meetings work.

Scriptable

Open source

The bar

Idle, it is a lozenge on the right edge of the screen. Hold the chord and it opens into a short bar of waveform that moves with your voice, and stays that size. Dots while it finishes, amber while the polish runs, a tick when it has pasted. Position and the idle tab are settings.

Meetings

Choose Record meeting, or let Speakhold offer when it notices an app on a call. Your microphone and what the Mac is playing are recorded as two streams, so you and the other side are labelled without a bot joining the meeting. Type your own notes while it runs; tap the chord once to mark a moment; ask for the last two minutes if you drifted. When the call app lets go of the microphone it counts down and stops on its own, or press stop. The rest happens in the background while you work: the menu bar shows how far along it is, dictation keeps working, and a notification opens the file when it lands. It transcribes on the Neural Engine, tells the far-side voices apart, names the meeting from your calendar, and writes Summary, Decisions, Action items and Open questions into one markdown file, with the full transcript underneath, in ~/Documents/Speakhold Meetings or a folder you choose. The audio is deleted.

Which model writes the notes. If Claude Code is on your Mac, the notes go through it under your own Claude plan, no API key and no API bill. Otherwise add your own Claude key and it reads the whole transcript; either is the one to use for a real meeting. Without a key, Apple Intelligence writes the notes on-device in parts, which is fine for a short call and weak on a long one, and the file says so. Either way the audio stays on the Mac; with Claude, the transcript text is sent with your key, nothing else.

How it works

Speakhold captures your microphone only while the chord is held, converts the audio to 16 kHz mono, runs it through Whisper Large v3 Turbo in-process, and types the result into the frontmost app with a synthetic ⌘V. The model (about 630 MB) downloads once on first launch. Polish runs on-device through Apple Intelligence. The only network request the app can make is the Claude engine, and only if you choose it and provide a key. Meetings add a Core Audio process tap for what the Mac is playing, behind macOS's "System Audio Recording Only" permission; the audio is transcribed locally and deleted.
